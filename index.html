How it works, privacy, and disclaimer

How it works

Taxing 212 reads Trading 212 history CSV files in your browser and produces figures useful for a UK Self Assessment return. It does not calculate how much tax you personally owe.

Capital gains

  1. Same-day buys and sells are grouped into single transactions under the same day rule.
  2. Same-day disposals are matched and gains calculated under that rule.
  3. Buys within 30 days of a disposal are matched under the bed and breakfast rule, unless you were not UK tax resident at repurchase.
  4. Remaining shares go into a Section 104 pool and are disposed using pooling rules.
  5. Each holding gets a trade log and a commented calculation ledger.
  6. A round-trips report shows which matching rule applied to each disposal in the tax year.
  7. A tax-year summary lists the figures typically needed for Self Assessment.

If a sale exceeds recorded holdings, check for missing history, in-specie transfers, corporate actions, or other data gaps before relying on the figures.

Dividends

Dividends are split into UK companies, other UK (funds/trusts), and non-UK. Foreign withholding tax is totalled for the selected tax year.

Privacy

Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is uploaded, no cookies are set, and there is no analytics. The code is open source on GitHub.
